# Demo storyboard

90 seconds. Record a local agent with a test account, or use the mock transcript in `examples/journey.md` and say on screen that the results are mocked. Do not show a real Connects balance, a real client name, or `org_uid`.

1. Title card, 5 seconds. "Upwork JobPilot Skills. Find better jobs. Write better proposals. Spend Connects smarter."
2. 10 seconds. Show the install command and the official MCP URL. Say the login is Upwork's, not ours.
3. 20 seconds. Prompt: find React and Next.js jobs. Show three rows with a reason and a risk. One row says Connects cost unknown.
4. 15 seconds. Prompt: analyze the first job. Show the score, an omitted dimension, and the words "not a chance of winning".
5. 15 seconds. Prompt: write a short proposal. Show a letter that names one project and does not contain a revenue claim.
6. 15 seconds. Prompt: apply, do not boost. Show the agent asking twice. Cut before a real confirm unless the account is a sandbox you are willing to spend.
7. 10 seconds. End card. Independent project. Not affiliated with Upwork. Link to the repository.

Do not add a view count, a star count, or a "win rate" caption.
