# Before and after

Both letters are fiction for a mock job: a clinic wants a Next.js booking page. The sample freelancer has one real project in the local profile, a scheduling app, and no revenue metric.

## Before

Hello, I am the perfect fit for this role. I am a passionate full-stack developer with cutting-edge skills in many frameworks. I can increase your revenue and start immediately. Please find my contact below and hire me.

## After

You need the booking page to let patients pick a time without calling the clinic. I shipped a scheduling app in Next.js that covered the booking flow and the staff calendar. I would start by mapping your current visit types to that flow, then wire the page to the calendar you already use. Which visit types have to be live in the first version?

The after letter does not add a revenue claim, a price, or an off-platform contact. Those were not in the mock profile.
